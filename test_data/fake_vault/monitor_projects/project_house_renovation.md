# Project House Renovation

## Goal
Renovate the house with a clear budget, timeline, and contractor plan.

## Scope
- Paint the living room and hallway
- Replace two windows and improve insulation
- Repair the back deck and reseal the stairs

## Budget
Estimate materials and labor, set a baseline budget, and hold a 10% contingency.

## Timeline
Create a timeline with weekly checkpoints and a final inspection date.

## Contractor Plan
Collect contractor bids, verify licenses, and lock a start date.

## Materials
List paint colors, flooring samples, and hardware options.

## Risks
- Contractor availability
- Scope creep
- Budget overruns

## Next Actions
Draft a week-by-week checklist and confirm the contractor schedule.
